# Pitchee 隐私政策

**生效日期：2026 年 11 月 8 日**

Pitchee 是一款完全在设备本地运行的声音分析与练习应用。我们的原则很简单：

> **我们不收集任何数据。** 你的声音、录音、分析结果和使用习惯全部保留在你自己的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器，不会共享给任何第三方，也不会用于任何跟踪或分析用途。

## 1. 我们收集哪些数据？

**没有。** Pitchee 不收集任何个人信息或使用数据，包括但不限于：

- 不收集姓名、邮箱、账号等身份信息（应用无需注册或登录）；
- 不收集位置、通讯录、设备标识符；
- 不集成任何统计、广告或崩溃上报 SDK；
- 不进行跨应用或跨网站跟踪。

App Store 的「App 隐私」标签与随应用分发的隐私清单（`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`）均声明：**不收集数据、不跟踪**。

## 2. 麦克风与录音

Pitchee 需要麦克风权限来录制你的声音，以便进行音高、音域等分析。

- 所有声音分析均在你的设备本地完成；
- 录音文件仅临时保存在应用沙盒内，并启用系统级文件加密保护；
- 录音**永远不会**上传到服务器或共享给第三方；
- 你可以随时在系统设置中撤销麦克风权限。

## 3. 本地存储

应用设置、练习记录等数据仅存储在你的设备本地，同样启用文件加密保护，并被排除在设备备份之外。删除应用即可删除这些数据。

当你主动导出报告图片时，应用会申请「添加到相册」权限，仅用于把你选择导出的图片保存到你的相册。是否导出、导出什么，完全由你决定。

## 4. 第三方链接

应用内提供了 GitHub 仓库、Discord 社区等外部链接。这些链接由你主动点击后在浏览器或对应应用中打开，其隐私实践受各自服务条款约束，与 Pitchee 无关。Pitchee 不会向这些服务发送任何你的数据。

## 5. 儿童隐私

由于我们不收集任何数据，Pitchee 同样不会收集任何儿童（包括 13 周岁以下用户）的个人信息。

## 6. 本政策的变更

如本政策发生变更，我们会在本页面更新内容并注明新的生效日期。重大变更会在应用内或版本说明中提示。

## 7. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系：

<https://github.com/Project-Pitchee/Pitchee-iOS/issues>

# Pitchee Privacy Policy (English)

**Effective date: November 8, 2026**

Pitchee is a voice analysis and practice app that runs entirely on your device. Our principle is simple:

> **We do not collect any data.** Your voice, recordings, analysis results, and usage stay on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, shared with any third party, or used for tracking or analytics.

## 1. What data do we collect?

**None.** Pitchee collects no personal or usage data of any kind:

- No identity information such as name, email, or account — the app requires no sign-up or login;
- No location, contacts, or device identifiers;
- No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs;
- No cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Both the App Store "App Privacy" label and the privacy manifest shipped with the app (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`) declare: **Data Not Collected, no tracking**.

## 2. Microphone and recordings

Pitchee needs microphone access to record your voice for pitch and range analysis.

- All voice analysis happens locally on your device;
- Recordings are stored only temporarily inside the app sandbox, protected by system-level file encryption;
- Recordings are **never** uploaded to a server or shared with third parties;
- You can revoke microphone access at any time in system settings.

## 3. Local storage

App settings and practice history are stored only on your device, protected by file encryption and excluded from device backups. Deleting the app removes this data.

When you choose to export a report image, the app requests "Add to Photos" permission solely to save the image you selected to your photo library. Whether and what to export is entirely up to you.

## 4. Third-party links

The app contains external links to the GitHub repository, the Discord community, and similar destinations. These open in your browser or the corresponding app only when you tap them, and are governed by their own privacy policies. Pitchee sends none of your data to these services.

## 5. Children's privacy

Because we collect no data at all, Pitchee collects no personal information from children (including users under 13) either.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and note the new effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app or in release notes.

## 7. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Reach us via GitHub Issues:

<https://github.com/Project-Pitchee/Pitchee-iOS/issues>
